# Arabic handling: what is and is not validated (generated)

Generated by `python -m taadeel.report` from `results/arabic_stress.json` and `results/arabic_wikisource_probe.json`. Do not edit by hand.

**No permitted real Arabic legal text is held, so nothing here is a validation on UAE law.**

## Synthetic stress set (test strings, not legislation)

37 cases covering diacritics, tatweel, alef and yaa variants, Arabic Indic and Persian digits, «مكرر», mixed Arabic and English, and invisible format characters (`src/taadeel/arabic_stress.py`). The locked v1 normaliser passes 25 of 37; on article labels 11 of 17. Its gaps: right to left and zero width marks, the byte order mark, the Arabic letter mark, Farsi yeh and keheh, ornate parentheses and a dash before «مكرر». `normalize_v2.py` pre cleans these and passes 37 of 37, but it was written after seeing the cases, so that score is by construction. It is not the default.

## One real Arabic law, counts only

Arabic Wikisource, Federal Law No. 7 of 2002 (oldid 137670), read under its robots.txt; its reuse status is not clear, so no wording is committed (`docs/DATA_SOURCES.md`). On a local copy the parser found 50 articles numbered 1 to 50 with 0 missing; a renumbering variant (real text, synthetic renumbering) aligned 250 of 250 articles with v1 and 250 of 250 with v2. One wiki transcription is a smoke test, not a validation set.

## Not validated

* Any real UAE federal law text, consolidated version or amending instrument.
* Arabic amendment by reference patterns (`amend_ar.py`): invented samples only.
* Effective date extraction from Arabic text; Hijri dates; Gazette references.
* OCR of scanned Arabic PDFs; the PDF extractor is tested on synthetic PDFs only.
* Search quality for Arabic (no clitic stripping or stemming).
