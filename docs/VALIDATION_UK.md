## Defects found during validation (disclosed)

The first full run and a hand check of a sample of recorded effects exposed three defects in the effects reader (`clml.py`), not in the locked engine. They were fixed and the evaluation re-run; the earlier result files are kept as `results/uk_validation_run1.json`, `run2` and `run3`.
1. A subsection effect such as `S. 14(2) repealed` was read as a whole section repeal.
2. A date such as `1.10.2007 for S. and 1.10.2008 otherwise` was resolved to the second date; the meaning of `S.` is not in the retrieved terms, so such dates are now ambiguous and excluded.
3. Northern Ireland only versions of a section with the same id were not dropped, giving duplicate section ids in three snapshots (the evaluator silently overwrote them). Duplicates now raise an error.

## Known limits

* Some recorded effects show no snapshot change on their date (examples are in `results/uk_handcheck.json`, including Theft Act 1968 s. 12A and s. 24A insertions and Misuse of Drugs Act 1971 insertions dated 1 July 1991). The cause was not established; one possibility, not tested, is that the site does not create a version at every effect date.
* Schedules are out of scope. Devolved material is out of scope. Prospective changes are excluded.
* Hand check by the project author's tooling, not a lawyer or an independent reviewer (files: `results/uk_handcheck.json`, `results/uk_handcheck_run1_sample.json`, script `uk_handcheck.py`, seed 20261012). A seeded draw of 20 disagreeing effects from the first run exposed defects 1 and 2 above. After the fixes, 10 seeded agreeing effects were read against their wording and all 10 matched, and the remaining disagreements (listed with the dates at which the section does change) were read; they are the unexplained cases in the previous bullet plus effects whose snapshot change falls on another date.
