# Brief for TII/ATRC

Focus: Arabic NLP and alignment rigour. Project: Taadeel (تعديل), a research and navigation tool for amendments to UAE federal legislation. It is not legal advice. The official Arabic text published by the competent authority prevails.

## Problem

A reader who asks what a law said on a given date, and what changed since, must today compare versions by hand. The technical core is article alignment: deciding which article of version B corresponds to which article of version A when articles are renumbered, inserted, repealed, reworded, or repeated with the suffix مكرر. Arabic adds orthographic variation (diacritics, tatweel, alef and yaa variants, Arabic Indic and Persian digits, invisible right to left marks) that breaks naive string matching. In templated laws every article resembles its neighbours, so similarity alone pairs the wrong articles. This is a measurable task. For Arabic UAE legislation the gold standard that would measure it does not yet exist.

## What exists

* Engine v1 (parse, normalise, align, diff, point in time), hash locked: the sha256 of each engine file is recorded in the result files and a run aborts if a file changed.
* Alignment v2, a separate module. It keeps the text similarity of v1 and adds number offset evidence from anchor pairs and abstention (a pair is taken only when its weight exceeds a threshold). Its weights were chosen on development seeds 0 to 29 only. A robustness rule was added after seeing development results and before any new seed was run.
* Normalisation v2, a pre clean step in front of v1, written after a synthetic Arabic stress set exposed gaps in v1. It is not wired into the parser or the store.
* Evaluation protocol: predictions are committed before a run, held out seeds are used once, result files refuse a second run, and misses are reported with the hits (`docs/PREDICTIONS_V2.md`, `docs/HELDOUT_RESULTS.md`).
* English and Arabic amendment by reference pattern modules. The Arabic lexicon is tested on invented samples only.

## Measured evidence

Scope is stated on every line. None of it concerns UAE law.

**Synthetic, v1, first held out run.** 55 of 60 point predictions fell within the pre registered tolerance. This is a calibration count, not an accuracy. 5 of 6 directional claims held. In templated near duplicate laws v1 recall was 0.710 for repealed and 0.700 for inserted articles, and 0.379 and 0.376 where many articles were inserted or repealed.

**Synthetic, v1 against v2, new held out seeds 920000 to 920099, same documents, run once.**

v2 did not meet the pre registered bar (claims not held: V1, V6): it raised repealed and inserted recall in every templated scenario and matched v1 on the UK pairs, but was slightly worse than v1 on the easy scenario (lowest v2 metric there 0.987). v1 stays the default engine.

Point predictions for v2: 57 of 60. Directional claims for v2: 8 of 10; claims missed: V1, V6.

## What is NOT validated

* Nothing is validated on UAE law or on real Arabic legal text. No real UAE law is loaded. The official portal returned HTTP 403 on robots.txt; this is recorded and was not worked around. A permission request is drafted and has not been sent.
* UK numbers say nothing about UAE law, Arabic drafting or Arabic orthography in practice.
* Schedules are excluded from the UK evaluation. Devolved material and prospective changes are excluded.
* UK ground truth is editorial, not a legal original. Hand checks were done by the author, not by a lawyer.
* Open research questions: a real Arabic gold set for alignment; repeated articles (مكرر) in real numbering; renumbering as practised in Arabic gazettes; amendment by reference in Arabic; effective date extraction.
