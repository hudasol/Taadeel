7. **Rule 6 was nearly exhausted; rule 6b added before fetching more.** Rule 6 found only 11 sections not already in the first sample (the first sample had taken 48 of 59). Most recorded effects on the 7 Acts cite a schedule paragraph ("s. 159(3), Sch. 10 para. 48"), whose wording is in that paragraph, not in the section. Rule 6b, declared after seeing only these counts and before fetching any paragraph: for each effect made by a UK Public General Act, take the first "Sch. N para. M" in its citation, fetch that enacted schedule paragraph, order by sha1 of the URL, cap 150. Count at declaration: 99 paragraphs carrying 197 effects; 12 schedule citations without a paragraph number are excluded. One paragraph (Employment Rights Act 2025, Sch. 10 para. 48) was fetched once to check the URL form and the XML structure before this rule was written; its wording was not used to change any pattern.
8. **Alignment v2 design and weight selection (M2).** `align_v2.py` is a new module; `align.py` is untouched. It keeps v1's text similarity and monotonic search, and adds number offset evidence from anchor pairs and abstention (a pair is taken only when its weight exceeds tau). Weights were searched on dev seeds 0 to 29 only (360 settings, `tune_v2.py`). The unconstrained best setting could pair two articles only with number evidence, which would fail on laws renumbered in ways the anchors do not explain. A robustness rule was therefore added after seeing dev results only and before any new seed was run: a pair with similarity 0.9 must be able to match on text alone. The selected setting loses 0.0008 of the dev objective. The background correction feature received weight 0 and is inactive.
