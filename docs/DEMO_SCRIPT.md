# 60–90 second demo script

Use the synthetic demo only and say so. Do not describe it as real legislation.

**0:00** Open the app. Point to the banner: "Taadeel is a research tool, not legal advice. The Arabic official text prevails." Point to the badge: "This demo is synthetic data, labeled on every response."

**0:15** Switch the view to Real. "No real law is loaded, because the official portal has not granted permission to copy its text. That is a deliberate rule, not a bug."

**0:25** Back to the demo. Drag the timeline slider across the amendment date. "As-of view: what the text said on a date, with source and retrieval date on every provision. If an effective date were uncertain, it says so instead of guessing."

**0:45** Scroll to Changes. "Article-level, word-level redline. Inserted, replaced, repealed, renumbered, and clarified are classified separately, and a low-confidence match is flagged instead of forced."

**1:05** Show `docs/VALIDATION.md`. "On hard synthetic cases, near-duplicate articles are the weak spot, and the report shows the numbers. Real-world accuracy is unmeasured until a permitted gold set exists."

**1:20** Close: "The engine is built and tested. Next: permission, extraction, amendment parsing, and a cited gold set."
